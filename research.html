Research

My research examines how firms coordinate internally and with supply chain partners, and how AI and algorithmic tools are reshaping procurement, negotiation, and business education. I draw on both qualitative and quantitative methods to understand how trust, governance, and technology intersect in high-stakes decision-making. Across these projects, I am especially interested in translating theoretical insight into practical guidance for managers, educators, and policymakers navigating increasingly complex, technology-mediated environments.

Published Papers

FLEX your MBA: Empowering Learning Efficiency in the Digital Classroom Using an AI-based Application

Shah, P., Mishra, I., Amarasuriya, S. Decision Sciences Journal of Innovative Education. Published

This study examines how an AI-based learning application affects engagement and learning efficiency among MBA students in digital classrooms, offering guidance for instructors integrating AI tools into graduate business education.

Chips and Challenges: An Integrative Review on Sustainability and Risk Management in Semiconductor Supply Chains

Mishra, I., Amarasuriya, S., Samani, M. N. Corporate Social Responsibility and Environmental Management. Published

This paper synthesizes literature on sustainability and risk management practices unique to semiconductor supply chains, highlighting key vulnerabilities and offering a framework for building more resilient, responsible sourcing strategies in a high-stakes industry.
