Research

My research examines how firms coordinate internally and with supply chain partners, and how AI and algorithmic tools are reshaping procurement, negotiation, and business education.

Current Research Streams

1. Transactive Memory & Relational Governance

My dissertation and related work develop a nomological network of transactive memory systems and examine how relational governance shapes intra- and inter-firm coordination, including buyer-supplier relationships in virtual work environments.

Transactive Memory Relational Governance

2. Algorithmic Buyers & AI in Procurement

I study how algorithmic and AI-based buyers change trust, commitment, and negotiation dynamics in procurement, including the limits of algorithmic negotiation and how continuance commitment differs when the counterpart is human versus algorithmic.

Algorithmic Negotiation Procurement

3. Sustainability & Risk in Supply Chains

My work reviews sustainability and risk management practices in complex, high-stakes supply chains such as semiconductors, and uses machine learning to map corporate supply chain networks.

Sustainability Risk Management Machine Learning

4. AI & Pedagogy in Business Education

I research how AI tools affect learning efficiency and academic discourse in MBA classrooms, and design experiential pedagogy, including podcast-based and case-based methods, for teaching procurement and operations.

AI in Education Pedagogy
