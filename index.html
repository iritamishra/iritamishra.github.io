Irita Mishra, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Management
Department of Marketing and Management, University of Wisconsin – River Falls

I am an Assistant Professor of Management specializing in supply chain and logistics management. My research examines intra- and inter-firm coordination — including transactive memory systems, relational governance, and buyer-supplier relationships — and how AI and algorithmic tools are reshaping procurement negotiations and business education. I received my Ph.D. in Logistics & Supply Chain Management from Georgia Southern University in 2023.

My work has appeared in Corporate Social Responsibility and Environmental Management and the Decision Sciences Journal of Innovative Education, with several papers under review at journals including the Journal of Supply Chain Management and the Journal of Purchasing and Supply Management.
