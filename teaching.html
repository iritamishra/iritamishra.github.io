Teaching

Courses taught at the University of Wisconsin – River Falls and Georgia Southern University, along with teaching certifications and evaluations available upon request.

Assistant Professor, UW – River Falls

MNGT 745: Artificial Intelligence for Business

MBA · Summer 2025 – present (2 sections)

Explores how AI and its sub-domains, including machine learning and deep learning, are being applied to real and emerging business problems, covering core AI terminology, practical uses and limitations, and the ethical implications of AI in business and society.

MNGT 744: Fundamentals of Data for Business

MBA · Spring 2024 – present (3 sections)

Covers the fundamentals of working with data for business decision-making, including pulling data from databases with SQL, cleaning and documenting it, and applying best practices for acquiring, describing, and managing data from a range of sources.

MNGT 705: Operations Management

MBA · Fall 2024 – present (3 sections)

Introduces the concepts and tools behind producing world-class goods and services, organized around three themes: core operations management principles such as strategy, logistics, and scheduling; project management; and quality management.

MNGT 706: Project Management

MBA · Fall 2024

Introduces the project management process, from selecting and defining a project through estimating time and cost, developing and executing schedules, minimizing risk, and closing it out, while building general skills for managing teams, timelines, and performance. Content is aligned with the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK) that underlies the CAPM certification exam.

MNGT 361: Operations Management

Undergraduate · Fall 2023 – present (11 sections)

Examines the operations function that every organization, public or private, profit or nonprofit, relies on to produce goods and deliver services, covering both the quantitative and qualitative sides of decision-making in manufacturing and service operations.

MNGT 362: Logistics and Transportation

Undergraduate

Introduces the logistics and transportation systems that power modern supply chains and global trade, covering routing, distribution, freight operations, and the technology used to move goods efficiently while balancing cost, risk, and sustainability. Emphasizes real-world applications and problem-solving skills relevant to careers in logistics, operations, and transportation management.

MNGT 389: Supply Chain & Logistics Management

Undergraduate · Fall 2025 – present (3 sections)

Introduces students to the fast-paced, interconnected world of supply chains and the skills needed to manage the flow of goods, information, and finances from raw materials to the final customer, with topics spanning procurement, inventory optimization, warehousing, and transportation management.

MNGT 300: Management & Organizational Behavior

Undergraduate · Fall 2023, Spring 2024 (2 sections)

Covers foundational management principles alongside the individual and group behavior dynamics that shape organizations, with the goal of preparing students to manage effectively in today's workplace.

Adjunct Lecturer, Georgia Southern University

OSCM 3430: Operations & Supply Management

Spring 2021 – Spring 2023 (5 sections)

Applies core supply chain management principles to the design, planning, control, and ongoing operation of systems across both manufacturing and service settings.

OSCM 4438: Negotiation

Spring 2023

Examines negotiation strategy and practice from a demand-driven, value-added perspective on performance management, building practical skills for negotiating effectively in supply chain and business contexts.

LOGT 2232: Introduction to Supply Chain

Fall 2020

A foundational introduction to the integrated management of supply and demand within and across organizations, covering the planning and execution of sourcing, procurement, production, distribution, and logistics activities, and how technology supports these functions and drives organizational performance.

Graduate Teaching Assistant, Georgia Southern University

OSCM 4436: Supply Chain Analytics

Spring 2022

OSCM 4431: Supply Management

Applies core supply chain management principles to the design, planning, control, and ongoing operation of systems across both manufacturing and service settings.
